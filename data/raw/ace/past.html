April, 2019

In April, we granted $1.39 million to a diverse group of 48 charities, organizations, and individuals from around the world8. Our goal for this round was to direct a substantial portion of our funding towards capacity-building projects, and we met this goal by funding 42 capacity-building proposals. We also hoped to fund a significant number of proposals supporting legal and policy work. We did not receive as many proposals as we hoped we would in that area, and we funded only 9 of them. Of the remaining grants, 31 are funding projects aimed at influencing public opinion, 9 are funding projects aimed at influencing industry, and 17 are funding projects aimed at building alliances. More information about each of the recipients, our research team’s evaluation and selection process, and future grant cycles can be found in our official announcement and grantee updates.
